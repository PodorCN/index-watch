Data & methodology

Returns
USD adjusted-close ETF proxies, including split and distribution adjustments. Periods start at the last close before the week, month or year.
Unusual moves
Window log return minus prior daily mean × sessions, divided by prior volatility × √sessions. Up to 63 baseline days; at least 40 required. A ranking heuristic, not a significance test.
Updates
Refresh checks completed closes and recalculates signals. Automatic checks run on opening and every 30 minutes while visible. Sources may be cached for 30 minutes. Unavailable sources retain dated snapshots; checks are not live quotes.
Indicators
Cboe volatility and FRED rates/spreads retain their own dates. Yield and spread changes use bp, not investment returns.
Private markets
Low-frequency references stay outside daily rankings. Listed proxies do not reproduce private NAV returns.
